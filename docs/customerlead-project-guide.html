02 · Lead lifecycle

What a lead means here

A lead is one customer inquiry: who contacted you, what they need, where the inquiry came from, and what happens next. It can be a sales opportunity, a quote request or an existing customer's support issue.

Example: Elvie emails “My shower keeps dripping.” The system creates one lead with her email address, original message, service/subject, AI category, priority and summary. An AI reply may be attached for review.

Field or screenMeaning
Lead_IDStable identifier for the inquiry. Form IDs start LEAD-. Gmail IDs use GMAIL- plus the incoming message ID.
StatusNew, Pending, Contacted, Qualified, Won, Lost or Closed. The owner can update it; confirmed manual sending moves New/Pending to Contacted.
Priority / categoryUrgency and type of request. These help organise work; they do not promise a booking, price or response.
InboxThe original inquiry and its linked reply. This is another view of the lead, not a second lead.
ContactsGroups customer details from inquiries. One person can have several leads.
Follow-up permissionExplicit permission for one automatic follow-up while the inquiry remains eligible.

Five emails from one person can produce five leads. Each message can describe a different problem. Gmail labels and message IDs prevent repeatedly processing the same captured message.

The status, draft review, sync status and delivery status are separate: Approved is an owner decision; Applied confirms external write-back; Sent requires a Gmail send result.

05 · Form journey

How a website inquiry becomes a lead

  1. The customer opens the original workflow's FORM — Customer Inquiry production URL, or a website form is connected to that intake.
  2. They provide name, email, phone, service, message and a Yes/No follow-up choice.
  3. The workflow validates the fields. It suppresses the same recent inquiry within the configured 15-minute duplicate window.
  4. When AI classification is enabled, Gemini classifies the service intent and produces a summary. The safe fallback preserves the lead if optional classification fails.
  5. SHEETS — Append Customer Lead writes the Leads row. Form captured → Publish Form captured reports that record to the CRM.
  6. The automatic-email master switch and individual switches decide whether to send a customer acknowledgement and/or owner alert. These gates are independent.
  7. SHEETS — Save Initial Email Results records Sent, Disabled or a failure result. The delivery publisher updates those fields in the dashboard.
  8. A consented, open inquiry can later qualify for one follow-up. No permission means no automatic follow-up.

What appears in the frontend: a lead in Leads and the original message in Inbox. A form classification alone does not create a support reply draft; use Create reply draft when replying manually.

Production versus test: Publish activates the production form and schedules. Execute is for a one-time test. In the Form Trigger, the Test URL is for a listening test execution; customers use the Production URL after publication.

A customer-facing form on a separate website still needs its submission wired to the intake. Merely installing the owner dashboard does not embed a public form on that website.

06 · Gmail journey

What happens during an inbox scan

  1. The published START — Every 5 Minutes schedule reads the dashboard's shared settings.
  2. Gmail — Find Unhandled Customer Emails searches the connected business mailbox. Your base query is:
in:inbox newer_than:2d to:adamromas0634@gmail.com -from:me

The search node also excludes AI-SUPPORT processing/outcome labels and drafts. to: selects the business recipient; from: would restrict who sent the email.

  1. Messages are handled one at a time. Sender screening rejects automated/bounce/no-reply/sent messages, and a processing label prevents overlapping handling.
  2. When AI is enabled, Gemini or OpenAI produces a suggested reply and classification. Missing primary-provider connections were repaired so fallback routing is available.
  3. When AI is disabled, the inquiry still reaches manual review without a fabricated reply.
  4. A valid reply creates a Gmail draft, addressed to the customer. The email publisher sends the inquiry and draft to the CRM. The no-draft path also publishes customer inquiries.
  5. The CRM automatically queues the inquiry/draft for the Sheet bridge. Leads and ReplyReviews then receive matching records.

Expected delay: up to the next five-minute scan, plus AI processing and the next minute-based Sheet sync. The visible dashboard refreshes every 30 seconds and when it regains focus.

Already labelled messages are not reprocessed just because you refresh the dashboard. Use a new test email to test capture again. The current newer_than:2d setting only searches the last two days.

13 · Reply review and delivery

Approve, sync, then confirm sending

  1. Open a real inquiry in Inbox. Choose Review reply draft or Create reply draft.
  2. Check the original request, recipient and subject. Edit the message to match your verified business facts, then save.
  3. Choose Approve reply. This records review and queues the review row; it does not send.
  4. Check Automation → External updates. Wait for the lead and draft updates to show Applied.
  5. Choose Send via Gmail. Review From, To, subject and the exact message in the confirmation dialog.
  6. Choose Confirm and queue email. The minute-based sender waits for all relevant sync changes, then claims the request once.
  7. The sender prepares/records the Gmail draft, performs the send and reports the returned Gmail message ID.
  8. Check Inbox → Outgoing replies for Sent, then verify the recipient inbox and Gmail Sent.

After confirmed delivery, a New/Pending lead becomes Contacted. Its thread/status update is queued for Sheets. A pending automatic follow-up is cancelled before a manual reply can be claimed.

Uncertain delivery: a Sending or Needs Review result must be checked in Gmail before another attempt. Send requests are not automatically reclaimed or blindly retried. Sent confirms Gmail accepted the send; it is not a read receipt.

If you cancel before the sender claims the job, nothing is sent by that request. Any follow-up already cancelled by confirming the manual reply stays cancelled.

14 · Follow-ups

One consented follow-up after 24 hours

A follow-up is a separate scheduled message. It is not the AI support draft and it is not the form receipt.

Required conditionWhy it matters
Automatic emails + follow-ups enabledBoth dashboard gates must allow the action.
Consent = YesThe customer explicitly allowed one follow-up. An incoming email alone does not grant permission.
Status = New or PendingContacted, Qualified, Won, Lost and Closed inquiries are excluded.
Follow_Up_State = PendingAlready reserved, sent, skipped or uncertain work is not selected again.
Due timestamp has passedThe configured delay is 24 hours by default; the hourly scan runs on or after that due time.
  1. The original workflow scans Leads hourly.
  2. It reserves an eligible row before attempting the Gmail send and reports Reserved to the CRM.
  3. After the attempt it records Sent and a UTC sent time, or Needs Review if delivery cannot be confirmed.
  4. The outcome publisher updates Follow-ups, Calendar and Activity in the frontend.

Why Gmail leads often show zero follow-ups: they start with Consent No and Follow_Up_State Skipped. That is expected. Use a separate consented form inquiry to test follow-ups.

Marking a lead Contacted, revoking permission or cancelling its pending follow-up blocks future eligibility after the change reaches Sheets. An already Reserved action needs execution review rather than a new schedule.

For a controlled quick test, use only a fresh test row you own and move its due time into the past. Do not reduce the business-wide delay or change unrelated rows just to test the scheduler.

Current local scope

This installation is a single local workspace. A hosted SaaS version needs user authentication, workspace/tenant isolation, a durable production database, secured public endpoints and reliable background hosting. The current local file should not be treated as a completed multi-tenant SaaS database.
